Splendid Catering — Homepage Hero Video
======================================

Place your licensed hero footage in this folder:

  public/assets/videos/hero-catering.mp4     (required for video playback)
  public/assets/videos/hero-catering.webm    (optional, smaller/faster in some browsers)
  public/assets/videos/hero-catering-poster.jpg  (optional; shown while loading)

Recommended export:
  - H.264 MP4, 1080p or 720p, 24–30fps
  - Target under 8–15 MB (compress with HandBrake or similar)
  - Muted loopable edit (no audio required on web)
  - Duration: 15–45 seconds looping seamlessly

If hero-catering.mp4 is missing, the homepage uses a cinematic poster
image (featured gallery photo from the database) with a slow Ken Burns effect.

After adding files, clear browser cache and reload the homepage.
